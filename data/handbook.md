15. Disciplinary Process a. Case Referrals
Each ill-disciplinary case shall be reported to the Registrar/Additional Registrar/Manager Academics. Those referring cases are usually supposed to serve as a complainant and to present appropriate proof before the Proctors / ITU Discipline Committee in disciplinary hearings. A disciplinary hearing is a formal method carried out by the Proctors / Disciplinary Committee of the
